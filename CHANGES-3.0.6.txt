DSE ERP 3.0.6

Quotation enhancements:
- GST and discount are numeric text fields.
- GST and discount auto-populate from item_master after item selection.
- Removed quotation item summary/footer controls.
- Added semantic icons to all item table headers.
- Made quotation detail values larger, bold, and visually highlighted.
- Improved mobile/contact visibility and removed the redundant bottom Close Details button.
- Prevented deletion of ACCEPTED quotations.
- Quotation number generation now scans existing numbers and always advances to an unused number, even after deletions.
