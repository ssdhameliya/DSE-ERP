DSE ERP 3.0.7 — Unified Sales Invoice Payment

Implemented
- Rebuilt Sales Record Payment as one unified screen.
- Invoice overview, payment entry, live invoice summary, payment status,
  quick actions, payment history and invoice timeline are now on one page.
- Full, partial and advance payment modes remain supported.
- Payment proof upload supports PDF, PNG, JPG and JPEG files up to 5 MB.
- Files are copied into the application configuration folder under:
  PaymentProofs/<invoice-number>/
- Stored payment proof paths are persisted with each payment record.
- Users can open stored payment proof files later from Payment History.
- Added drag-and-drop payment-proof selection and Browse File support.
- Payment history includes date, mode, reference, payer, amount, status,
  notes, proof-view action and row actions.
- Added payment-mode and date-range filtering plus CSV export.
- Invoice totals, paid amount, balance, payment percentage and status refresh
  immediately after saving a payment.
- Invoice timeline reflects invoice creation, email status, WhatsApp status,
  latest payment and current balance.
- Quick actions include invoice PDF generation, printing, invoice email,
  WhatsApp receipt handoff, payment receipt email, statement export and invoice view.
- Existing databases are upgraded additively with received_from,
  payment_type and attachment_path fields.
- Application version remains 3.0.7 as requested.

Validation
- RecordPayment.fxml parsed successfully as XML.
- Modified Java source braces are balanced.
- Database migration hook and new payment fields are present.
- Full Maven compilation was not run because Maven is unavailable in this environment.
