JASVI SALES INVOICE RELEASE
===========================

Scope
-----
- Rebuilt the Sales Tax Invoice PDF using the approved JASVI Industries layout.
- Added the approved JASVI header as a bundled project resource.
- Preserved purchase, quotation, return, database, email and WhatsApp behavior.

Sales invoice improvements
--------------------------
- JASVI header, TAX INVOICE title and ORIGINAL FOR BUYER indicator.
- Invoice number, dates, payment terms, reference and currency.
- Billing and delivery address blocks populated from transaction data.
- Dynamic line-item table with no artificial blank rows.
- Automatic page continuation for larger invoices.
- Taxable amount, CGST/SGST or IGST, round-off and grand total.
- Amount in words, bank details, UPI QR image, terms and authorized signature.

Verification
------------
- Maven clean package: passed with JDK 25.
- Document pipeline: 25 sales, 8 purchases and 8 quotations generated.
- Generated sales invoice rendered to PNG and visually checked against the JASVI reference.
