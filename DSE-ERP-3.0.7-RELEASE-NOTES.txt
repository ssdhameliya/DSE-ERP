DSE ERP 3.0.7

Payment workflow
- Combined Record Payment and Payment History into one screen.
- Saving a payment now refreshes invoice totals and history without navigating away.
- Added payment-mode filtering and CSV export directly on the Record Payment screen.
- View Payment History now focuses the embedded history table.
- Payment summary remains visible beside the form.

Reminder Center
- Replaced the misleading reminder/settings-style action glyph with a clear ellipsis actions icon.
- Existing Edit, Complete, Snooze, Reopen, and Delete menu actions remain available.

Communication Center
- Replaced the icon-only action with a visible “Resend Email” button.
- The button is displayed only for EMAIL records.
- Clicking it regenerates/locates the selected quotation, sales, or purchase PDF and sends it to the selected record’s recipient.
- Successful resend activity is written back to communication_log.

Validation
- Updated FXML files were parsed successfully.
- Modified Java files have balanced source braces.
- Full Maven compilation was not run because Maven is not installed in this environment.
