DSE ERP 4.0.7 - Tax Invoice Layout & Pagination

- TAX INVOICE bar aligned to full invoice content guides.
- Invoice/order and invoice-date/PO-date rendered as separate equal cards.
- Transporter and contact-person rendered as separate equal cards.
- Terms and signature cards changed to equal-width layout.
- Pagination now measures actual iText-rendered item-table height, including multi-line Item Master Remarks.
- Removed fixed row-count pagination that could leave one record on the first page.
- Multi-page distribution is balanced by measured rendered height.
- Final page uses a fixed-height item region with blank grid filler rows so bank details, calculations, INR/Grand Total, terms, signature and footer remain anchored to a stable page position.
- No sales, tax, database, email, settings, or item-mapping business logic changed.


4.0.7 FINAL PAGINATION / TRANSPORT / ALIGNMENT REFINEMENT
- Bank/Calculation and INR/Grand Total standardized to 49/2/49 geometry.
- Transport card now shows Transporter, Delivery Method (Yes/No), and Vehicle Number.
- Contact card now shows Contact Name and Contact Number with +91 display formatting for 10-digit Indian numbers.
- Removed balanced page splitting. Non-final pages now fill to actual measured rendered-height capacity.
- Blank filler rows are rendered only on the final page so closing sections remain anchored.
- Footer group moved slightly lower while retaining the same position for single and final multi-page invoices.
