# What's New in DSE ERP v10.0.36

### System-Wide Date & Time Format Parity
- **360 Profiles Parity:** Customer 360 and Supplier 360 tabs (quotations, invoices, payments, follow-up dates, internal notes, document uploads) strictly respect configured company date and time patterns.
- **E-Way Bill & E-Invoice Compliance:** E-Way Bill Center and GST Compliance registers display dates formatted according to company settings.
- **Document & PDF Parity:** Sales Tax Invoices, Purchase Orders, Branded Registers, and Unified Business Reports format all document dates and timestamps per company settings.

### Quotation & Sales Experience
- **Customer Dropdown Optimization:** Fixed customer dropdown clipping and single-item display on initial click; lists now display smoothly with full visible row counts.
- **Source Selection Parity:** Quotation source field is clean on creation and avoids auto-defaulting to "Direct".

### Dialog Ergonomics & Visual Polish
- **Dynamic Dialog Sizing:** Responsive sizing and scrolling prevent clipping of chips, action buttons, timeline nodes, and preview controls across Audit Trail, Bank Entries, and Thermal Print views.
- **Barcode & QR Order Dispatch Validation:** Replaced free-text entry with a searchable dropdown restricted to active, valid Sales Invoices and Purchase Orders to eliminate duplication and manual entry mistakes.
- **Register Table Headers:** Credit Note and Debit Note registers updated with canonical purple Actions column header and icon.
